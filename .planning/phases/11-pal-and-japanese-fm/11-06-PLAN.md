<objective>
Add the owned `vdp_vtable` fixture: a Z80 program that samples the V counter once per line over a whole frame on PAL and NTSC at every screen height and reports closed-form summaries of what it saw (D8).

Purpose: REG-02 asks for PAL fixtures of the V-counter tables with equal hashes on all eight lanes. The tables are proven in-process already (`tests/vdp/test_timing.c`); this fixture adds the path a program sees through `IN A,($7E)` and the frame-level numbers every lane must agree on. The next plan turns the records into lane hashes.
Output: one assembly source built three ways, its digests, 33 or so literal expectation rows, twelve tests with controls, a short guide section.
</objective>
