<objective>
Build the fixture that proves GUN-04 on a real Z80 program: a known lit square, a dark square and a band, polled at ten loop
lengths for TH and for the latched X, judged against a closed form derived in cmake and never read from the core (CONTEXT
decision 10), with the controls that must fail and the runner's half of "aim through the crop" (GUN-03). Then the second hosted
dispatch, the first hosted view of the fixture.

Purpose: the Z80-visible edge, delay and sticky bit are tested end to end at lengths that lock a purely geometric sensor to one
column, and a core regression that moves the latch fails a row instead of passing its own pin.
Output: `light_phaser.sms`, its rows, nine tests, one cross-lane hash row, the dispatch record.
</objective>
