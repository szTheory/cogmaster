window, no core call and nothing hashed (CONTEXT decision 9, "Hosts"; GUN-03). The libretro adapter of the next plan is its only
client; the runner uses none.

an off-by-one in the 1-based conversion or a forgotten crop offset fails a unit test, not a game.
