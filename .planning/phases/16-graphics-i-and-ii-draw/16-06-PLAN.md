## Decisions taken in this plan (with reasons)

- **State before the sprite engine (D-26 order, D-18):** D-26 lists sprites before state 17. The sprite engine's fields
  are saved state, and rule 4 forbids instance state outside the record for even one commit, so the record and the
  revision bump land in the plan just before the engine. The push cadence of D-26 is unchanged: the second push (plan 08)
  comes after the renderer and the sprites, with the state change already in it, and the third after the docs. The new
  order is recorded under D-26 in 16-CONTEXT.md as a plan-time sequencing refinement for the owner to see; reordering
  plans 06 and 07 reverses it.
- **Separate 4-slot struct (D-18 fallback):** not needed; the 16520 pin research computed holds for the shared array.
- **`sprite_overflow` carries two meanings, documented where it is declared and where it is loaded:** in mode 4 it is a
  0/1 flag (an overflow pending for the line); in legacy modes it is 0 or 1 plus the SAT index of the fifth match (1..32),
  which plan 07's scan stores and the line's first dot copies into `fifth_index`. The comment on the field in
  `src/internal.h` and the comment at its class-dependent check in `src/state.c` both say so.
- **The `src/state.c` header comment:** its offsets list (line 34, "behaviour revision 16") names the header's byte offset
  16, not a revision number, so it stays as it is; the Layout paragraph's VDP clause gains the three fields appended after
  VRAM.

Scope note (10 files, accepted): the record, the struct, the status read, the header constants, the two tests that pin
the format number and the classified field list, the state test and its registration, and the two inventories; the
format change cannot land in fewer files without a commit whose record misses a field.
