<objective>
Add the five own-fixture `audio@300` rows and make the fixtures judge check each row's full identity (region, tv, fm) against what the runner actually ran (D6).

Purpose: REG-03: FM rows are judged by their sound, and `fm` is part of the row identity. A hash that carries no profile bytes is only meaningful with its identity pinned in the row and checked against the run, and the default for `fm` lives in three places (the awk, the core, the runner) that only this check keeps equal.
Output: five rows, the 9-row fixtures judge with the identity and no-flag checks, ratchet audio cases, the page counts, the policy and conformance sentences.
</objective>
