Wire the SN76489: Z80 writes to $40-$7F reach a lazily caught-up PSG that emits attenuation-coded levels into the
synth; the PSG resets with the audio reset, restarts its step origin with the tick count and snapshots its levels
through the synth; its state is saved; and every tone, volume and noise setting is proven with closed-form oracles.

Purpose: SND-01's unit half and the PSG half of the amended SOFT-reset rule.
Output: `src/psg.c`, `cogmaster-test-psg`, the reset and state cases, the closed-form cases.
</objective>
